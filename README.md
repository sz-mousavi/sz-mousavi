# Seyedeh Zahra Mousavi

Molecular geneticist and bioinformatician at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Munich, Germany.

I work on **multi-omics in cancer biology and male infertility**, combining whole-exome sequencing, transcriptomics, proteomics, and structural modelling to explain disease mechanisms and support translational research.

## Focus

- **Genomics:** WES (GATK4), CNV detection
- **Transcriptomics:** microarray, bulk RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, circRNA
- **Proteomics and extracellular vesicles**
- **Networks:** WGCNA, cell–cell communication
- **Structural analysis:** protein modelling, docking, MD simulation
